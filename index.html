Frequently asked.

> Do I need training or experience?

No. We're not looking for polished performers. We're looking for real people with genuine passion, personality, and something to say. Training comes later. Authenticity comes first.

> Can I apply from any country?

Yes. This is a global project. The 7th Room has no borders. We're looking for girls from all over the world. That's the whole point.

> What should my video look like?

Be yourself. Sing, dance, rap, create. Whatever feels most like you. It doesn't need to be professionally filmed. A phone recording in your room is perfectly fine. We care about who you are, not production quality.

> When will I hear back?

We review every single application. If your signal resonates, we'll contact you via email within 4-6 weeks. Please check your inbox and spam folder regularly.

> Can I apply in multiple categories?

Choose the one that feels most natural. If you truly are an all-rounder, select that option and show us your range in your video. One application per person.

> What happens after I am selected?

Selected applicants enter the next phase. Deeper conversations, creative evaluations, and eventually, entry into the 7th Room. We'll guide you through every step.

> Is there an age limit?

You must be born in 2005 or later. If you're under 18, parental consent will be required during later stages.

> What language should I apply in?

English is preferred for the application form, but your video can be in any language. Express yourself in the way that feels most natural to you.
